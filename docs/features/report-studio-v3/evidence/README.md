# Report Studio V3 — release-candidate evidence

**Commit:** `dddb4c061079e88484b1585dce6c6ed863a8e01e` (the final head).

**Where it ran.** This commit was built into the production **standalone Docker image** (`frontend/Dockerfile`).
The image ran as a dedicated container, and every scenario used that running image. The backend was isolated,
with a temporary Postgres holding the Olist and "E2E presentation sales" datasets. The planner and the visual critic
used the real model.

**How it was produced.** `e2e/acceptance/report-studio-v3.spec.ts`, run as
`npx playwright test -c acceptance.config.ts` with `E2E_BASE_URL` pointed at the container. Traces were recorded for
every scenario (`trace: 'on'`); they are regenerated by the same command and are too large to commit.

**What `results.json` holds.** Every assertion that ran, the timings, the model's notes, and the export's own record
of itself (`s8_export`).

**Status rule.** A scenario stays **NOT VERIFIED** until at least one of its assertions runs. A skip, or a model that
returned no design, is never recorded as a pass.

## What the images and the PDF show

These observations come from the images and the rendered PDF pages, reviewed one by one.

- **KPI scope (DoD 1).**
  - Beside a headline about Jan–Aug 2018 (R$ 7.4M), the Revenue KPI's R$13.6M now says what it covers:
    "All periods · Sep 2016 – Sep 2018". On a phone it shortens to "Sep 2016 – Sep 2018".
  - Where the tile has room, the change is stated with its own window and value: "Jan 2018–Aug 2018 · R$ 7.4M ▲ 137%
    vs same months 2017". It no longer appears as a bare "+137%" under the total.
  - Incomplete months are drawn as a faded dashed segment, labelled "(incomplete period)", so the last month no longer
    reads as a collapse to zero.
- **Facts are not problems (DoD 2).** Operations titles concentration and incomplete periods "Worth knowing".
  "Against target" appears only where the data has a target.
- **S3 visual critic.** The critic ran on the settled preview, with at most 2 calls. The re-check reports the repairs
  it could see and names what is still visible. None of it is claimed as fixed.
- **S5 structure.** The result has the reference's hierarchy: a headline band, the KPI strip, an explanatory summary,
  then the full-width argument chart and the paired charts. The model's disclosure is shown to the author:
  - converted: KPI strip, full-width hero chart, summary block;
  - approximated: serif editorial style;
  - not supported: custom fonts beyond serif.

  **Not reproduced:** the reference's dark header band and its warm cream page surface. The native theme stays light.
- **S6 filter.** Filtering to SP moved the KPI from "R$13.6M" to "R$5.2M" and moved every narrative sentence with it
  ("R$ 7.4M, +137%" → "R$ 3.0M, +164%"). No sentence was left stale.
- **S8 export.** The PDF is taken from the published report. It runs to 6 sheets, broken between grid rows, with no
  warnings page. The exporter's own record is `warnings: []` and a minimum print scale of 0.75 of screen size. Sheet 1
  holds the scoped headline and KPIs; sheet 6 holds the full Top sellers table, legible.
